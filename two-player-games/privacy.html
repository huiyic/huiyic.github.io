Privacy policy

2 Player Games - Arcade · Effective date: 9 October 2026

This app is published by Jun Xiang Lee.

Playing the games

The app runs locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data through gameplay. No account is required. The app does not include advertising, tracking or developer-operated analytics. Games can be played without an internet connection.

Scores and settings

Match state and session win counts remain on your device during the current session. Session wins can be cleared with Reset score in the game library. No player profile or cloud save is created by the app.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information only to respond to your request. Support correspondence is handled separately from gameplay. Contact us at the address below to request deletion of correspondence. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing junxiang.lee@tabitalk.com. Do not send sensitive information.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store purchases, downloads and device diagnostics under its own policies and your device settings. These platform services are separate from the app’s local gameplay.

Children

The app does not request personal information from children or offer user chat or public profiles.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy when our practices change. Contact junxiang.lee@tabitalk.com with privacy questions or requests.
