2 Player Games - Arcade

Play together

Choose a game, then select Play vs friend. Sit at opposite short ends of one phone: blue plays from the bottom and coral from the top. The top player’s controls face them. Shared-board games announce whose turn it is.

Play solo

Select Play vs bot. You control blue; the app controls coral. Touches cannot control the bot.

Pause and rematch

Use the pause icon at the screen edge during a match. After a match, choose Rematch or return to the library. Reset score clears session wins. Use Music on/off in the library to toggle its music.

Report a problem

Email junxiang.lee@tabitalk.com. Include your device model, iOS version, game name and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful, but remove personal information first. Jun Xiang Lee will respond via this address.
