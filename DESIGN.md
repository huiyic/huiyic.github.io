# Design direction

## Mosaic editorial portfolio

The homepage is a calm, high-contrast personal portfolio that borrows the reference site's confidence and image-led rhythm without copying it. The first viewport pairs a direct, oversized thesis with an asymmetric collage of product notes, interface fragments, and personal observations. A thin vermilion accent is the only saturated color.

### System

- **Palette:** chalk white `#fafaf8`, ink `#11110f`, muted gray `#6b6a65`, hairline `#deddd8`, vermilion `#d83424`.
- **Type:** Manrope for display and titles; DM Sans for body and navigation.
- **Composition:** generous shell, crisp 1px rules, asymmetrical collage, compact navigation, editorial writing index.
- **Interaction:** restrained color and translate-on-hover affordances; smooth anchor scroll; visible keyboard skip link.
- **Responsive behavior:** collage becomes a stacked, still-asymmetrical field below 800px; writing rows keep date/title/arrow scanability.

### Content rules

Lead with current truth (“Product Manager at Amazon”). Do not invent Amazon work, project metrics, testimonials, or dates. Present verified side projects as full-width editorial features, beginning with Furusato Calculator; preserve the writing archive as the durable proof of thinking.
